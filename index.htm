About

What is Khanapara Teer?

Khanapara Teer is a legal archery-based lottery game regulated by the Meghalaya Amusements and Betting Tax Act. The game is played in two rounds every working day.

Archers shoot a fixed number of arrows at a target. The last two digits of the total number of arrows that hit the target form the result.

Round 1
10:30 AM (Morning)
Round 2
3:30 PM (Evening)
